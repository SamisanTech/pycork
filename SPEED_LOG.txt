OUTER HULL  (new feature: cork had no working outer-hull for a single self-
overlapping mesh -- BoolProblem only classifies two labelled operands by
parity, so winding number 2 regions were mislabelled "outside").
================================================================================
Implemented cork/mesh/mesh.hull.tpp + pycork.outerHull(verts, tris,
raysPerPatch=5, resolve=True) -> (verts, tris, stats).

Algorithm:
  1. group faces into patches across manifold (2-face) edges; intersection
     curves / open edges separate patches
  2. per patch, sample seed faces from the LARGER faces only (slivers on top
     of another sheet give unreliable ray counts); cast rays both ways
     through the SI uniform grid, require w- == w+ + 1, classify
     keep(w+=0) / flip(w-=0) / delete
  3. majority vote per patch; large patches with several dissenters are split
     by multi-source BFS (nearest seed wins)
  4. topological vote fixes small patches: along every intersection edge
     exactly two faces must survive -> forces slivers in/out
  5. prune leftover small non-manifold-bounded components
